# L1 Assessment — AI-Claims Practical Exam

**Goal:** demonstrate you can tell confident-correct from confident-guessing AI without
checking every claim — the L1 "move on when" bar.

## The exercise

1. Pick a codebase or domain you know well.
2. Ask AI a set of ~10 questions where you already know the right answers — mix easy,
   obscure, and "edge of its knowledge" questions, and slip in 2–3 questions with false
   premises.
3. For each answer, **before** verifying, predict: is this correct, or confidently
   guessing? Note the signal you used.
4. Verify each answer and score your predictions.

## Passing bar

- You correctly flagged the hallucinations and the false-premise questions.
- You can articulate *what tipped you off* (vagueness, over-specific detail on rare facts,
  refusing to say "I don't know") — not just that you got lucky.
- You map at least one failure to one of the four properties (Next Token Prediction,
  Knowledge, Working Memory, Steerability).
