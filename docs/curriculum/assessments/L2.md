# L2 Assessment — One-Week PR / Session-Log Audit

**Goal:** show that reviewing diffs and pushing for simplification is now a *habit*, not a
one-off — the L2 "move on when" bar.

## The exercise

For one week, keep a lightweight log of your AI-assisted work. For each PR or significant
session, record:

- What you asked for and what the AI produced.
- At least one thing you **caught in review** that you'd otherwise have merged.
- At least one place you said "this is overcomplicated, simplify" and got a better result.
- Whether the change was surgical (every line traces to the request) or had drive-by edits
  you pruned.

At the end of the week, audit the log against the **SPACE** framework — a balanced view of
developer productivity, not just output volume:

- **S**atisfaction & well-being
- **P**erformance (quality of outcomes)
- **A**ctivity (volume of output)
- **C**ommunication & collaboration
- **E**fficiency & flow

## Passing bar

- The log shows consistent diff review (not just on the hard PRs).
- At least three concrete catches and three simplification wins across the week.
- A short reflection using SPACE: did AI improve *outcomes and flow*, or just raise activity
  volume? Honest "where it slowed me down" notes count in your favour.
