# L5 Assessment — AI as Architecture Partner

**Goal:** demonstrate the L5 stance — using AI to challenge your own assumptions, not just to
do work you've already decided on.

## The exercise

Take a **real, consequential decision** you're facing (an architecture choice, a tech-stack
call, a design trade-off) and use AI as a thinking partner against your own instinct:

1. Write down your current position and the assumptions behind it.
2. Use anti-sycophancy prompting to make AI argue *against* your position — steelman the
   alternative, surface the risks you're discounting, attack your assumptions.
3. Apply the evaluator–optimizer pattern: have AI critique and improve the strongest version
   of each option.
4. Decide, and document what changed.

## Passing bar

- You can point to at least one assumption AI surfaced that you had not consciously
  examined.
- The final decision is better-reasoned for the exercise — either you changed your mind, or
  you can now defend your original choice against the strongest counter-argument.
- You actively countered sycophancy (you did not let AI simply agree with you), and can show
  the prompt(s) you used to do it.

Attach your before/after notes and the key prompts as evidence.
