## 3. Set your real name in GitHub

📝 **Small request: set your real name in GitHub**

When you sign in to the tracker, we see your GitHub account. To help us know who's who (and give you the right support), please make sure your GitHub **profile name is your real name** — not just a nickname or handle.

**How to do it (takes 30 seconds):**
1. Go to **https://github.com/settings/profile**
2. In the **Name** field, type your real name — e.g. `Mykhailo Melnyk`
3. Click **Update profile**

⚠️ **One important step:** after updating your name, please **log out of the tracker and log back in**. The new name only shows up for me after you re-login — so this quick step makes sure I can see who you are.

Your username/handle can stay whatever you like — we just need the **Name** field filled in so it's clear who's on the path.

Thanks, everyone! 🙏
