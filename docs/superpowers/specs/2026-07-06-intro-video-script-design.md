**[Beat 1 — Hook · ~0:30]**
`[on-screen: title card — "Agentic Engineering at Solvd" + your name/role]`

Hi everyone. I need five minutes of your time. I want to share something important for this
year. And then I will give you one small thing to do this week.

Here is the simple truth. AI is not a side tool anymore. It is now part of how we build.
Everyone has AI today. So the real difference is not who has it. The difference is who
**practices** with it every day, and who only tries it sometimes. This program is here to
help all of us practice.

**[Beat 2 — Our role · ~0:30]**
`[on-screen: three icons — "The path" · "The tracker" · "Doing it with you"]`

So what is our job here? Three things. First, we made **the path** — a clear way to go from
"I use AI sometimes" to "AI makes me much faster." Second, we made **the tracker** — this is
where you follow the path and mark what you finish. And third — this is the important part —
**we are doing this with you.** We are not here to test you or judge you. We are all learning
this together.

**[Beat 3 — The five levels · ~1:15]**
`[on-screen: the 5 levels as a vertical ladder]`

The path has five levels. The names explain them.

**Level 1 — Understand.** Use AI to *read*, not to write. You learn how it thinks. And you
learn when it is right, and when it is only guessing.

**Level 2 — Edit with Review.** Quality is the goal. You read every change the AI makes. You
can look at its code and say "this is too complex — make it simpler." *[pause]* Please
remember this one: **Level 2 is the goal for most of us.** If you reach Level 2 and it feels
natural, you are exactly where this program wants you to be.

**Level 3 — Plan and Implement.** You always make a plan before you change many files.

**Level 4 — Orchestrate.** You set up tools that check your work for you, and your team
shares them. This makes the whole team faster.

**Level 5 — Architecture Partner.** You use AI to question your own ideas — not only to do
the work, but to test if your plan is good.

You do not need to rush to the top. Levels 3, 4, and 5 are there when you want more.

**[Beat 4 — The habit that gets you there · ~1:00]**
`[on-screen: "Small steps every day"]`

So how do you move through these levels? Not in one long weekend. The best way to get good is
simple: **use AI on your real work, a little, every day.**

Each step is small. Ask AI to explain a file you already know — this helps you see when it is
wrong. Take one unclear prompt and make it clear, then compare the results. Read one change
before you accept it. That is enough. Fifteen minutes every day is better than one long day
once a month. This is a skill. Skills grow with practice.

The tracker helps you keep the habit. You can see your levels fill up. You can feel that you
are making progress.

**[Beat 5 — How the tracker works · ~1:15]**
`[on-screen: screen-share of the tracker — sign-in, path picker, a level with checkboxes, the ⚑ button]`

Let me show you. It is very simple. *[switch to screen share]*

Open the tracker and **sign in with GitHub**. One click. That is your account. The first time,
**pick your path** — Web, Mobile, or Backend — so the tasks match your real work. *[point]*

Then you see your levels and the tasks. When you finish one — a reading, a short course, or a
practice task — you **check the box.** *[check one]* That is the whole thing. It saves right
away. It is your progress, and no one is watching each box.

One more thing. Do you see this small flag? *[point to ⚑]* If a link is broken, or you have an
idea to make the path better, click **Report / suggest**. It comes straight to me. Please send
it — your feedback makes the path better for everyone.

*[switch back to camera]*

**[Beat 6 — The ask · ~0:30]**
`[on-screen: big and simple — "This week: 1) Sign in  2) Pick your path  3) Start Level 1" + tracker URL]`

So here is the one thing I ask. It is small. **This week: sign in, pick your path, and start
Level 1.** That is all. Do not wait for free time. Do the first task today. Then do one small
step tomorrow.

I am doing this with you. The feedback flag comes to me. And I am happy to see where we go
together. Let's build the habit. Thank you, everyone.

`[on-screen: end card — tracker URL]`
