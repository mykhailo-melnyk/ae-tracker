How the wall works

How often does the wall update?
It's recomputed at most every ~5 minutes, so a task you just ticked can take a few minutes to appear here.
What does each card mean?
  • Top movers — the most tasks completed in the last 7 days.
  • Milestones — crossed a task-count landmark (10, 25, 50, 75, 100) in the last 7 days.
  • Leveled up — finished every task in a level, with the final one completed in the last 7 days.
  • Cert-ready — completed all required items for a certification in the last 7 days.
  • Longest streaks — the most consecutive weeks (Mon–Sun) with at least one completed task.
  • Just started — completed their first-ever task in the last 7 days.
  • Welcome back — completed a task after being away 2+ weeks.
Why do some cards say "last 7 days" but streaks talk about weeks?
Most cards use a rolling 7-day window (the last 7×24 hours). Only the streak card counts calendar weeks (Monday–Sunday), because a streak is about showing up week after week.
Can I appear on more than one card?
Yes — the cards aren't mutually exclusive. Finish a level during a busy week and you might show up on several at once. 🎉
Why do only 8 names show per card?
Each card lists up to the top 8. More people may qualify — the cap just keeps the wall readable.
Why isn't there a full ranking of everyone?
By design, the wall only shows positives — there's no bottom of the list, no completion percentages, and no full leaderboard. You can only ever appear on it, never be ranked last.
Why am I not on the wall?
You'll show up the moment you hit any of the above — tick a task, keep a streak alive, or finish a level. Keep going!
